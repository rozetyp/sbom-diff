Why Use This Tool?

Fast, private, and powerful analysis right in your browser.

100% Private

All processing happens in your browser. Your SBOMs are never uploaded to any server.

Powerful Diffing

Quickly see components that were added, removed, or changed between two versions.

License Policy Checks

Define and enforce license policies to automatically flag forbidden licenses (e.g., GPL, AGPL).

Broad Format Support

Natively parses and compares both CycloneDX and SPDX JSON formats, no conversion needed.

Your Data Never Leaves Your Device

This tool is built on a foundation of privacy. We don't have servers to upload your data to, even if we wanted to. All parsing, diffing, and policy checking is performed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. Your sensitive SBOMs stay with you.
